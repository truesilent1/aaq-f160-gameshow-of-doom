Descend. Revise. Survive.

Three floors of five rooms each. Four rooms are multiple-choice monsters and the fifth is a boss that you answer in writing, just like the exam. You start with three hearts and no help at all.

HeartsA wrong answer costs a heart. A failed boss costs two. Run out and the run is over.
BossesWritten answers marked against a mark scheme. Defeat one to heal a heart and choose a new lifeline.
LifelinesNone at the start. Each boss offers three to pick from: Peek, Ask the class, 50:50, Shield, Second Wind or Cheat sheet.
SavesYour run saves as you go, so you can close the page and continue later.
